How points work
+3 quick +2 steady +1 slower −1 wrong spelling

Long words give you more time; the timer bar shows how long you have for each one. Each round has 10 words. Get 8 right to unlock the next room. Win the chase for a second star, and win it with 9 or more right for three stars. A word you get wrong comes back later for a second try.

British spellings are shown (colour, centre). If you type the American spelling (color, center), that counts too.
